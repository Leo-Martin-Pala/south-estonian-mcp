# Mulgi: Overview and Orthography

## Identity & Five Subvarieties
- **Language code:** `mulgi` (Mulgi; *Mulgi kiil*)
- **Western South Estonian:** Spoken traditionally across five parishes (**Karksi**, **Halliste**, **Helme**, **Tarvastu**, **Paistu**). Internal variation across these five varieties is authentic: **never reject a valid form simply because another parish uses a different ending** (Helme in particular can reflect Tartu/Võro influence).
- **MCP Support:** Very rich lexicon (`eki_mulgi_dictionary`, `eki_ems_dictionary`, `smugri4_mulgi_dictionary`, `eki_vms_dictionary`), substantial monolingual corpus (`uitsainus_mulgimaa`, `murdekorpus_mulgi`, `mulgikeelne_taheke`), LLM Estonian parallel corpus, offline language classifier (`mulgi`), and Mulgi-tailored leakage linter (which permits `-b/-p` 3sg, `-me` infinitives, `-n` inessive/participles, and `-ss`).

## Orthographic & Phonological Hallmarks
1. **No Glottal Stop (`q`):** Unlike Võro and Seto, Mulgi has **lost the word-final glottal stop** (`q` / `'`). Words ending in `-q` in Võro/Seto are normally endingless or vocalic in Mulgi (e.g., *latse* "children", *ole* "be", *tiidä* "to know"). Do not insert Võro `-q` into Mulgi!
2. **Initial `h`-loss:** Word-initial `h` is routinely absent in traditional Mulgi spelling (`obese` = "hobused", `ain` = "hein", `obu` = "hobune", `äste` = "hästi"). **This is authentic speech/orthography, NOT extraction damage.**
3. **Vowel Syncope / Stem Alternation:** Quantified stems alternate prominently across cases (`kiil / keele / kiilt`, `kuul / kooli / kuuli`, `süük / söögi / süüki`, `puut / poodi / puuti`).
4. **Vowel Harmony (Front vs Back) & Reduced *õ*:** Back vs front harmony applies generally, though western Mulgi subvarieties show transitional features toward Estonian compared with Võro/Seto; *õ* is often restricted to stressed/initial syllables or alternates in unstressed endings.
