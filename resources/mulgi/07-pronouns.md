# Mulgi Pronouns (Asesõnaq)

## Pronoun Classes

1. **Personal (isikulise asesõnä):**
   - 1sg: `mea` ~ `mia` ~ `ma` (gen. `miu` / `mu`)
   - 2sg: `sea` ~ `sia` ~ `sa` (gen. `siu` / `su`)
   - 3sg: `temä` ~ `ta` (part. `tat` / `tedä`)
   - 1pl: `me` ~ `mede`
   - 2pl: `te` ~ `tede`
   - 3pl: `nemä` ~ `nevä` ~ `na`
2. **Reflexive (enesekotuselise asesõnä):** `esi` ('self'), `ende` ('of self'), `esiende` ('of oneself')
3. **Reciprocal (vastastikuse asesõnä):** `tõ(i)netõise` ('each other'), `üitstõise` ('one another'); partitive `üitstõist`, `tõ(i)netõist`
4. **Possessive (omasteve asesõnä):** `oma` ('own')
5. **Demonstrative (näitäve asesõnä):** `sii` ('this/that'), `nii` ('these/those'), `siisama` ('the same'), `seante` ~ `siande` ~ `sihant(d)e` ('such'); note that general South Estonian `too` / `taa` do not occur in modern Mulgi (archaic `tuu` is attested only in very old speech)
6. **Interrogative (küsive asesõnä):** `ken` ~ `kes` ('who'), `mis` ~ `mih` ~ `mes` ('what'), `kumbas` ('which of two'), `meante` ~ `miande` ~ `mihant(d)e` ('what kind'), `midäs` ('what, why'), `kun` ~ `kus` ~ `kos` ('where'), `kunkotte(a)l` ('whereabouts')
7. **Relative (siduve asesõnä):** `ken` ~ `kes`, `mis`, `kumbas`, `meante` ~ `miande` ~ `mihant(d)e`
8. **Indefinite (umbmääräse asesõnä):** `kennigi` ('someone'), `mõni` ('some'), `mitu` ('several'), `mõlembe` ~ `mõlepe` ~ `mõlempe` ('both'), `muu` ('other'), `pallu` ('much/many'), `üits` ('one'), `üitsigi` ('not a single'), `kik(k)` ('all'), `egä` ('every'), `egäüits` ('everyone'), `terve` ('whole'), `esi` ('self'), `kumaki` ('either')

## Interrogative Pronoun Declension

| Case | `kes` ('who') | `mis` ('what') | `kumb` ('which of two') |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Nominative** | `kes` ~ `ken` | `mis` ~ `mih` ~ `mes` | `kumbas` |
| **Genitive** | `kelle` | `mike` (Krk) ~ `mille` (Trv) | `kumba` |
| **Partitive** | `kedä` | `mikest` ~ `midä` ~ `mes` | `kumbat` ~ `kummat` |
| **Illative** | `kelles` | `mikes` ~ `milles` | `kumbas` ~ `kummas` |
| **Elative** | `kellest` | `mikest` ~ `millest` | `kumbast` ~ `kummast` |
| **Comitative** | `kelleg(e)` | `mikeg(e)` ~ `milleg(e)` | `kumbage` ~ `kummage` |

## Compound Interrogative + Demonstrative Forms

Mulgi frequently combines interrogative and demonstrative pronouns (written hyphenated, often fused in speech):
- `kes-sii?` (`kessi?`) — 'who is this?'
- `kes-nii?` (`kesni?`) — 'who are these?'
- `mes-sii?` (`messi?`) — 'what is this?'
- `mes-nii?` (`mesni?`) — 'what are these?'

*Parish abbreviations: Krk = Karksi, Trv = Tarvastu.*
