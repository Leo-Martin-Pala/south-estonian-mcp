# Mulgi Nominal Declension Paradigms (24 Principal Classes)

Mulgi nominals (approx. 7,300 classified lemmas, ~110 subtypes) group into **24 principal declension classes** based on Karksi (West Mulgi) and Tarvastu (East Mulgi) patterns. A leading dot (`.`) marks overlength (Q3).

## Gradation Patterns

| Pattern | sg.n | sg.g | sg.p | sg.ill | pl.g | pl.p | Model Types |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Strengthening QG (VV) | W | S | W | S | W ~ S | S | `kirme`, `mõte` |
| Strengthening QualG (LV) | W | S | W | S | W ~ S | S | `ammas`, `tüdär` |
| Weakening QG (VV) | S | W | S | S | S | S | `aam`, `tükk` |
| Weakening QualG (LV) | S | W | S | S / Q3 | S | S | `aab`, `mägi` |
| No gradation | – | – | – | – | – | – | `võle` |
| Non-gradational w/ Q-shift | Q2 | Q2 | Q2 | Q2 | Q2 | Q3 | `kipe` |
| Non-gradational w/ Q-shift | Q2 | Q3 | Q3 | Q3 | Q3 | Q3 | `paise` |
| Non-gradational w/ Q-shift | Q2 | Q2 | Q3 | Q2 | Q2 | Q3 | `naine` |
| Non-gradational w/ Q-shift | Q1 | Q1 | Q3 | Q3 | Q1 | Q1 | `tuli` |

### Qualitative Gradation Subtypes
- **Assimilation / dissimilation:** `rs:rr` (`kõrs:kõrre`), `rd:rr` (`pird:pirru`), `nd:nn` (`ind:inna`), `l'g:l'l` (`jäl'g:jäl'le`), `ld:ll` (`kuld:kulla`, `sül'd:sül'lä`), `mb:mm` (`ammas:.amba`).
- **Replacement:** `s>vv` (`kuus:kuvve`), `b>v` (`varb:varva`), `b>vv` (`kuub:kuvve`), `d>vv` (`aud:avva`), `g>vv` (`lõug:lõvva`).
- **Loss / insertion:** `d/t>Ø` (`jõud:jõu`, `täht:tähe`), `b>Ø` (`uba:ua`), `g/k>Ø` (`rügä:rüä`, `tuhk:tuha`), `s>Ø` (`käsi:käe`).

### Plural Partitive Vocalic Rules
- `-u`: `a`-stems with first-syllable `a, i, õ` (`vanu`, `vakju`).
- `-e`: `i`- and `u`-stems, and `a`-stems with first-syllable `o, u` (`käse`, `.puud'e`).
- `-i`: `e`- and `ä`-stems, and `a`-stems with first-syllable `oe` (`.lehti`, `.sulgi`).

## Group 1: Strengthening Gradation (4 Principal Types, ~15 Subtypes)

| Type | sg.n | sg.g | sg.p | sg.ill | pl.g | pl.p |

| `iste` | `iste` | `.istme` | `istet` | `.istmes(e)` | `istete` ~ `.istmide` | `.istmit` |

*Note: Weak-stem `pl.g` (`ammaste`, `kirmete`) is typical of West Mulgi (Karksi/Halliste); strong-stem `-ide` (`.ambide`, `.kirmide`) is typical of East Mulgi (Tarvastu/Helme).*
