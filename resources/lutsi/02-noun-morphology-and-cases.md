## Additional Attested Nominal Characteristics (`lutsi_org_paradigms` & `lutsi_org_examples`)
- **Glottal-stop plural (`-q` / `-ˀ`):** `māq` ("lands"), `Hobezaq olliq kyvaq.` ("The horses were strong."), `piniq rebäze kinni vytnuq` ("dogs would catch the fox"), `Kost sa neoq kalaq vytiq?` ("From where did you get those fish?").
- **Stem / Consonantal Samples (`lutsi_org_paradigms`):**
  - `üțș` (`üt̅’s`, "one"), `lațș` (`лat̅’s`, "child"), `āh̦` (`âHJ`, "goose"), `lumi` (`лumì`, "snow"), `nāņe` (`nān’e`, "woman"), `taļvine` (`tal’vìne͔`, "wintery"), `hybehane` (`he̮be͔hane͔`, "silvery"), `vyeras` (`ve̮eras̀`, "foreign"), `hameq` (`hamè͔ˀ`, "shirt").
- **Inessive `-hn` / `-h` / `-n` and Allative `-le`/`-lõ`:** Inessive `-h` (Pilda) / `-hn` (standardized primer) / `-n` (Nirza) aligns closely with Eastern South Estonian / Seto; never replace with Estonian `-s` or `-sse`.
- **Translative `-st`:** Formally merged with the elative `-st` (`māst`, `latsest`, `haļļast`, `sūrest`), never Estonian `-ks`.
- **Partitive plurals:** Vocalic / diphthongal stems such as `maid`, `kallu` (`ei ole-eiq kallu` = "nor fish"); never Estonian `-sid`.
