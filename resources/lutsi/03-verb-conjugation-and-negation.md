# Lutsi: Verb Conjugation and Negation

## Attested Verb Paradigms (`lutsi_org_paradigms`: `practical` / `UPA`)

### 1. Copula `olema` (`olē͔ma` = "to be")
| Form | Present (`practical` / `UPA`) | Past (`practical` / `UPA`) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1sg | `ole` / `oлe͔` | `olli` / `ol̆’li` |
| 2sg / 2pl | `olt` / `oлt̀` | `olliq` / `ol̆’liˀ` |
| 3sg | **`um`** / `um̅` | **`oļ`** / `ᵘol̅’` |
| 1pl | `olem` / `oлē͔m` | `ollim` / `ol̆’līm` |
| 3pl | **`ummaq`** / `um̆maˀ` | **`olliq`** / `ol̆’liˀ` |
| **Negated** | **`ole-eiq`** / `oлe͔i·ˀ` | **`ole-es`** / `ole͔e·s̀` |

### 2. Irregular Motion Verb `minemä` (`minēmä` = "to go")
- Present: 1sg `lǟ` (`lä̂`), 1pl `lǟm` (`lä̂m`), 2sg/2pl `lǟt` (`lǟt̀`), 3sg **`lätt`** (`lät̅`), 3pl **`lätväq`** (`lät̀väˀ`).

### 3. Additional Core Infinitive Classes (`lutsi_org_paradigms`)
`tegemä` (`tegēmä`, to do), `andma` (`aǹdma`, to give), `opma` (`op̅ma`, to study), `lugēma` (`лugē͔ma`, to read), `kirotama` (`kirot̆tama`, to write), `umblema` (`um̀ble͔ma`, to sew), `magahama` (`magahama`, to sleep), `küdzämä` (`küdzǟmä`, to bake).

## Core Verbal Rules (vs Standard Estonian)
- **3sg present:** Endingless or `-s` (`Repän nār` = "The fox is laughing"), `um` ("is") — never `-b`.
- **3pl present:** `-vaq`/`-väq` (`lätväq`), `ummaq` — never Estonian `-vad`.
- **Past Negation (`es` / `-es`) & Present Negation (`ei` / `-eiq`):**
  - Attested postposed forms: **`ole-eiq`** (pres: `"ei ole-eiq rebäst, ei ole-eiq kallu"` = "there are neither foxes, nor fish") and **`ole-es`** (past).
- **Active Past Participle (`-nuq` / `-nüq`):** `"piniq rebäze kinni vytnuq"` — never Estonian `-nud`.
- **Never normalize UPA (`oлe͔i·ˀ`, `um̆maˀ`, `ᵘol̅’`) or practical (`vytnuq`, `oļ`) into Estonian.**
