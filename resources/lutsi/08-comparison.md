# Lutsi Adjective Comparison

## Degrees of Comparison

- **Comparative suffix:** `-mb(i)`, `-mp`, `-p` (e.g. `paremb` ~ `parõmp` 'better', `sūremb` 'bigger', `vanemb` 'older').
- **Superlative:** formed analytically with `kyige` + comparative (e.g. `kyige paremb` 'best').

## Comparative Constructions

Lutsi attests both Finnic locational comparatives and particle comparatives (including Baltic-influenced negative-marker comparatives):

1. **Locational (partitive standard):**
   - `vanemb minno` ('older than me')
2. **Neutral particle `ku` ('than'):**
   - `pihlappuust um parõmp kuu tammõst vil` ('rowan is even better than oak')
3. **Negative-marker particle `ei`:**
   - `to oļļ sūremb ei şō` ('that one was bigger than this one')
4. **Negative marker + 'than' (`eigu`, `üskui`):**
   - `eigu` (< `ei` + `kui`) and `üskui` (< past negation `is` + `kui`)

## Adjective Agreement

Adjectives agree with their head noun in case and number (`nuoreq latseq` 'young children'), **except** in the comitative and abessive, where the modifying adjective stands in the genitive (`kahr sūre lāpagaq` 'a bear with a big paw').
