# Lutsi Pronouns

## Personal Pronouns

Lutsi distinguishes short and long (emphatic) forms in the singular (long forms in parentheses). Third-person pronouns have no grammatical gender (`tä` = 'he/she', `nǟq` = 'they').

| Case | 1SG | 2SG | 3SG | 1PL | 2PL | 3PL |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Nominative** | `ma` (`mina`) | `sa` (`sina`) | `tä` (`timä`) | `mī` (`mīq`) | `tī` (`tīq`) | `nǟq` (`nimäq`) |
| **Genitive** | `muq` (`minu`) | `suq` (`sinu`) | `timä` | `mī` (`mīq`) | `tī` (`tīq`) | `näide` |
| **Partitive** | `minnu` | `sinnu` | `timmä` | `meid` | `teid` | `näid` |
| **Adessive** | `mul` (`minul`) | `sul` (`sinul`) | `täl` (`timäl`) | `meil` | `teil` | `näil` |
| **Allative** | `mulle` (`minule`) | `sulle` (`sinule`) | `tälle` (`timäle`) | `meile` | `teile` | `näile` |

*Note: In 3sg adessive and allative, the long forms `timäl` and `timäle` are most commonly used.*

## Demonstrative Pronouns (3-Way Distance System)

Lutsi maintains a three-way demonstrative contrast: proximal `sjō` ('this'), medial `tā` ('that nearby'), and distal `tū` ('that yonder').

| Case | Proximal Sg / Pl | Medial Sg / Pl | Distal Sg / Pl |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Nominative** | `sjō` / `njōq` | `tā` / `nāq` | `tū` / `nūq` |
| **Genitive** | `sjō` / `näide` | `tā` / `naide` | `tū` / `noide` |
| **Partitive** | `setä` / `näid` | `tād` / `naid` | `tūd` / `noid` |

## Interrogative Pronouns & Question Words

| Case | Animate ('who') | Inanimate ('what') |
|---|---|---|
| **Nominative** | `kiä` | `miä` |
| **Genitive** | `kink` | `mink` |
| **Partitive** | `ketä` | `mitä` |

- **Yes/no question particles:** `kas`, `vai`
- **Local & manner interrogatives:** `kohn` ('where?', inessive/adessive), `kost` ('from where?', elative/ablative), `kohe` ('whither?', illative/allative), `kuis` ('how?')
