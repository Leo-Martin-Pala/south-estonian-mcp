## Compound Cardinals (Teens, Tens, Hundreds, Thousands)

- **Teens (`-tyiskümme`):** `ütştyiskümme` (11), `katştyiskümme` (12), `vīştyiskümme` (15)
- **Tens (`-kümmend`):** `katşkümmend` (20), `katşkümmend katş` (22), `kolqkümmend` (30), `vīşkümmend sǟdze` (57)
- **Hundreds (`-sada`):** `sada` (100), `sada vīş` (105), `katşsada` (200), `kolqsada nelikümmend kateza` (348)
- **Thousands (`-tuhat`):** `tuhat` (1,000), `katştuhat` (2,000), `katştuhat katşkümmend` (2,020), `kolqkümmend kūştuhat katşsada ütezäkümmend vīş` (36,295)

### Case Government
With `ütş` ('one'), the counted noun takes the **nominative singular** (`ütş latș` 'one child'). With all higher numerals (when the numeral is in the nominative), the counted noun takes the **partitive singular**: `katş last` ('two children'), `ütezäkümmend kolq last` ('93 children').
