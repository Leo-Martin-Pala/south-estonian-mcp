# Lutsi Inflection Tables

## Noun Declension (13 Cases)

Lutsi has 13 productive nominal cases (no separate essive; translative merges formally with elative `-st`). Inessive varies by village between `-hn`, `-h` (Pilda), and `-n` (Nirza); `-hn` is the standardized primer form. Vowel harmony governs the abessive (`-ldaq` after back stems `a, o, u, y`; `-ldäq` after front stems `ä, e, i, ö, ü`), while comitative `-ga(q)` does not harmonize.

## Verb Conjugation (Indicative Present, Past & Negation)

Lutsi preserves both South Estonian conjugations (Ø-conjugation: 3sg unmarked, 3pl `-vaq`/`-väq`; s-conjugation: 3sg `-s`, 3pl `-zeq`). Key features:
- **2sg and 2pl are syncretic** in both present (`-t`) and past (`-q`).
- **Past 2sg, 2pl, and 3pl all end in `-q`**.
- **Negation** is postverbal and cliticized with vowel assimilation and final-syllable stress; a single form serves all persons and both numbers (`-eiq`/`-aiq`/`-äiq` in present; `-es`/`-as`/`-äs`/`-is`/`-us`/`-üs` in past).
