# Võro: Overview and Orthography

## Identity & Family Position
- **Language code:** `vro` (Võro; Võrokiil)
- **Subgrouping:** Core Eastern South Estonian (`vro` and `seto` share close historical unity; Võro is the largest South Estonian variety with an active literary standard curated by the Võro Institute).
- **MCP Support:** Full dictionary (`synaq`, `smugri4_vasted_n`, `eki_ems_dictionary`, `eki_vms_dictionary`, `minecraft_vro`), monolingual & parallel corpus search, GiellaLT morphology/speller/grammar tools, offline NMT (`vro <-> et/en`), TartuNLP Neurotõlge, and offline language identification (`vro`).

## Writing System & Key Graphemes
1. **Glottal stop `q`:** Written word-finally as `q` (or apostrophe `'` / `’` in older/practical/Seto-influenced texts). It marks critical grammatical distinctions:
   - Nominative plural: `mõtsaq` ("forests"), `taloq` ("farms")
   - *da*-infinitive: `tetäq` ("to do"), `minnäq` ("to go"), `ollaq` ("to be")
   - Imperative 2sg: `võtaq` ("take!"), `tulõq` ("come!")
   - Present/past connegative: `ei tiiq`, `es olõq`
   - Some lexical stems also end in `-q` (e.g., `kastõq`, `teq`).
2. **High unrounded `õ` (`y` in older/academic texts):** Productive across both stressed and unstressed syllables via back vowel harmony (`kõnõlõs`, `olõ-õi`, `kirotõdas`).
3. **Vowel Harmony (Vokaalharmoonia):** Front vowels (`ä`, `ö`, `ü`, `e`, `i`) co-occur in native suffixes (`eläs`, `näeq`, `süvväq`), contrasting with back-vowel suffixes (`and`, `kõnõlõs`, `saanuq`, `mõtsaq`). Do not flatten `ä`/`õ` into Standard Estonian `a`/`e` (`midägi`, `täämbä`, not Estonian `midagi`, `täna`).
4. **Palatalization:** Acute mark (`ś`, `ń`, `ľ`, `ŕ`, `ḿ`, `ť`) in linguistic/dictionary notation, or apostrophe `'` (`olľ` / `oll'`, `tulľ` / `tull'`, `pańń` / `pann'`), or unmarked in broad practical spelling when context is clear. Palatalization often distinguishes **simple past** from present (`olľ` "was", `tull'` "came", `kõnõl'` "spoke").
5. **Vowel Raising / Length & Diphthongs:** Historical mid vowels raised in long stressed syllables: `kiil` ("language", Est. *keel*), `suul` ("salt", Est. *sool*), `kuul` ("school", Est. *kool*), `üü` ("night", Est. *öö*), `tüü` ("work", Est. *töö*), `hää` ("good", Est. *hea*), `pää` ("head", Est. *pea*), `tii` ("road", Est. *tee*).
6. **Spelling Legitimacy:** Forms such as `talo`, `elo`, `kodo` (retaining historical `-o` in non-initial syllables) are standard Võro — never "correct" them to Estonian *talu*, *elu*, *kodu*.
