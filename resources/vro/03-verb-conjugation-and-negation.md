# Võro: Verb Conjugation and Negation

## Two Present Conjugations (Critical Rule!)
Standard Estonian merges verbs under 3sg `-b`. Võro **never** uses `-b` for regular 3sg present (`-b` is an Estonian leakage error); instead, verbs split into two conjugations:
1. **Endingless / Vowel-stem conjugation (`tulõma`, `andma`, `olõma`):**
   - 3sg present is endingless: `and` ("gives"), `tulõ` ("comes"), `lätt` ("goes"), `tege` ("does"), `om` ("is", pl `ommaq`).
   - 3pl present ends **`-vaq` / `-väq`**: `andvaq`, `tulõvaq`, `tegeväq`, `lätväq`.
2. ***s*-conjugation (`elämä`, `kirotama`, `kõnõlõma`, `ütlemä`):**
   - **3sg present ends `-s`**: `eläs` ("lives"), `kirotas` ("writes"), `kõnõlõs` ("speaks"), `ütles` ("says"), `mõtlõs` ("thinks").
   - **Critical trap:** Do NOT misread `-s` present verbs as Estonian simple past (`elas`, `kirjutas`)! Look at the whole clause tense markers and vowel harmony (`ü` / `õ` / `ä`).
   - 3pl present ends **`-sõq` / `-seq`**: `eläseq`, `kirotasõq`, `kõnõlõsõq`.

## Past Tense, Conditional, Impersonal, Participles
- **Simple Past:** Marked by palatalization / gemination / stem vowel (`olľ` / `oll'` "was", `tull'` "came", `kõnõl'` "spoke"), `-i` (`kõnõli`, `anni`), or `-s` / `-si` depending on verb class; plural avoids Estonian `-sin/-sime/-site` (`-imiq`/`-siq`, `-itiq`/`-sitiq`).
- **Impersonal Present:** **`-tas` / `-täs` / `-das` / `-däs`** (`tetäs` "is done", `loetas` "is read", `kõnõldas` "is spoken") — never Estonian `-takse/-dakse`.
- **Conditional:** **`-si` / `-ssi` / `-ssiq`** (`olõssi` "would be", `teessi` "would do"), or compound past `-nu(q)` — never Estonian `-ksin/-ksime/-ksite`.
- **Active Past Participle:** **`-nu(q)` / `-nü(q)`** (`olnuq`, `saanuq`, `tennüq`) — never Estonian `-nud`.
- **Passive Past Participle:** **`-t` / `-d` / `-tu(q)` / `-tü(q)` / `-du(q)` / `-dü(q)`** (`tett` / `tetüq`, `loet`) — never Estonian `-tud/-dud`.
- **Supine (`ma`-forms) & Gerunds:**
  - Illative: `-ma` / `-mä` (`minemä`, `kõnõlõma`)
  - Inessive: **`-man` / `-män`** (`elämän`, `tüütämän`) or `-mah/-mäh` — avoid Estonian `-mas`.
  - Elative: `-mast` / `-mäst` (`elämäst`)
  - Abessive: **`-malda(q)` / `-mäldä(q)`** (`elämäldäq`) — avoid Estonian `-mata`.
  - Gerundive (`des`-equivalent): **`-dõn` / `-ten` / `-n`** (`kõnõldõn`, `visatõn`) — avoid Estonian `-des/-tes`.

## Negation (Present `ei` vs Past `es` + Postposed Clitics)
- **Present Negation:** Preverbal `ei` + connegative (`ei olõq`, `ei tiiq`, `ei kõnõlõq`) OR fused/postposed **`-õi` / `-i` / `-aiq`**: `olõ-õi` ("is not"), `võta-aiq` ("does not take").
- **Past Negation:** Preverbal **`es`** + connegative (`es tiiq` "did not do", `es olõq` "was not") OR postposed **`-õs` / `-as` / `-s`**: `olõ-õs`, `võta-as`.
- **Never** use Estonian contracted `pole`, `polnud`, `poleks`, `polevat`.
