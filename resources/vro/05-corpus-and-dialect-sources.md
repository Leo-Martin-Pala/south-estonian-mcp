# Võro: Corpus and Dialect Sources

The public corpus includes Vikipeediä (Võro Wikipedia), Võro fiction and the Võro–Estonian parallel corpus. The public dictionary includes IDS, Vikipeediä leads and Wikidata labels, and Väike murdesõnastik.

The noncommercial dataset also includes TartuNLP’s licensed Uma Leht edition, `laused_n` bilingual usage examples, and `vasted_n` phrase equivalents.

Use `get_language_stats` with this variety to check current source coverage.
Use the source URL and credit attached to examples when quoting them. Check
locality and spelling before treating a historical form as a modern standard.
An absent form may reflect limited source coverage.
