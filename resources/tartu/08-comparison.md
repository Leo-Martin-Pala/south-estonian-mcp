# Tartu Adjective Comparison & Adverbial Marking

## Comparative & Superlative

- **Comparative suffix:** `-mb` / `-mp` (shared across central/western South Estonian; e.g. comparative adverbial `ennembide` 'earlier', built on `ennemb` + Tartu `-de`).
- **Superlative:** analytical `kõige` / `kige` + comparative.

## Diagnostic Adjective & Adverb Morphology in Tartu

- **Manner adverbs in `-de`:** Tartu generalizes the `-de` illative suffix as a productive manner-adverb marker on adjectives:
  - `hirmsade` ('terribly; very much')
  - `kangede` ('strongly')
  - `ennembide` ('earlier')
- **Late gemination in southern Tartu adjectives:**
  - `likke` ('wet'), `jämme` ('thick')
- **Translative on predicate adjectives:** `-s` in general Tartu, `-st` in North & East Tartu (`sai pahazest` 'became angry', `jäi õnnetust` 'became unhappy', `jäi vanast` 'grew old', `sai tervest` 'recovered').
