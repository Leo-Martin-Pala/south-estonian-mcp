# Tartu Inflection & Morphology Reference

Tartu is the central South Estonian variety (spoken in four subdialect groups: North [Tartu-Maarja, Kavastu], East [Võnnu, Kambja], South [Otepää, Rõngu, Sangaste], and West [Rannu, Puhja, Nõo]), and served as the primary historical base of Old Literary South Estonian (17th–19th c.). Morphologically it is the most innovative South Estonian dialect group.

## Nominal Morphology

### Case Markers & Diagnostic Forms
- **Nominative plural:** historical plural marker lost in most of Tartu (endingless, like Mulgi); in **East Tartu** (Võnnu) the glottal stop `-q` is preserved (`miheq` 'men', `latseq` 'children'); in **Sangaste & Otepää** innovative `-ze` plural occurs (`küläze` 'villages').
- **Illative:**
  - Generalized **`-de` illative** across most of Tartu (`ladude` 'into the barn', `pudelide` 'into the bottle', `puulde pääde` 'halfway up'). The same `-de` suffix also forms manner adverbs (`hirmsade` 'terribly', `kangede` 'strongly', `ennembide` 'earlier').
  - **`-i` illative** for `-ne` and `-us` stems (`minestuzi` 'into a faint', `tõizi mõtsa` 'into another forest', `pahanduzi` 'into trouble').
  - **Võnnu (East Tartu):** lacks `-de` illative, using Võro-type **`-he`** (`kerikohe` 'into church', `koormahe` 'into the load').
- **Inessive & Essive merger:** both cases merge into **`-n`** (`tütrikun` 'in the girl / as a girl').
- **Translative:** `-s` in general Tartu; **`-st`** in North and East Tartu (`sai pahazest` 'became angry', `jäi õnnetust` 'became unhappy', `jäi vanast` 'grew old', `sai tervest` 'recovered').
- **Plural stems:**
  - **Strong-grade `i`-plural** is well preserved, especially in West Tartu (`lehtist` 'from leaves', `jalgul` 'on feet', `tähtile` 'to stars').
  - **North Tartu** (Tartu-Maarja, Kavastu) uses North-Estonian-aligned weak-grade `de`-plural (`loomadel`, `siadele`, `vennadega`) and `-sid` plural partitive (`taluzid`, `loomasid`, `kohtazid`).

## Demonstrative Pronouns

Tartu typically operates a **two-way demonstrative system** (with a third medial pronoun in the South group):
- **Proximal:** sg. `sii` ~ `si` ('this'), pl. `nii` ~ `ni` ~ `ne` ('these')
- **Distal / abstract:** sg. `tuu` ~ `tu` / `too` ('that'), pl. `nuu` ~ `nu` ('those')
- **Medial (South Tartu only):** `taa` ('that there')

## Verbal Morphology

### Person, Tense & Mood
- **Present 3sg:** predominantly **`-p`** (adapted from North Estonian `-b`: `tulep` 'comes', `annap` 'gives', `juup` 'drinks', `kannap` 'carries').
  - **East Tartu (Võnnu):** also uses Räpina-type `-ze` forms (`aap` ~ `aaze` 'drives', `juup` ~ `jooze` 'drinks').
  - **Southern Kambja:** preserves archaic endingless South Estonian 3sg (`joo`, `kand`).
- **Present 1pl / 2pl (South Tartu):** Võro-aligned `-mi` and `-ti` / `-di` (`saami` 'we get', `eläti` 'you live', `parandadi` 'you fix').
- **Present 3pl (South Tartu):** distinctive **double-marked** 3pl (`lääväve` 'they go', past `leiväve` 'they hit').
- **Past tense variants by subdialect:**
  - General / Kambja: **`i`-past** (`and'`, `jättivä`)
  - South Tartu (Otepää, Rõngu, Sangaste) & Southern Kambja: **`se`-past** (`astse` 'stepped', `võtsemi` 'we took', `andse`, `jätsevä`)
  - East Tartu (Võnnu): **`e`-past** (`ande` 'gave', `ole` 'was', `tahte` 'wanted')
- **Personal endings on `nud`-participles** (in indicative, conditional, and quotative plural):
  - `oleme saanume` ('we have received'), `oleks läänüti` ('you [pl.] would have gone'), `olevat kadunuva` ('they are said to have disappeared')
  - Standalone quotative participles also take personal endings: `kutsnude` ('you [pl.] are said to have called'), `pidänuva` ('they are said to have had to').
- **Personal endings on negated verbs:**
  - `ei annami` ('we do not give'), `ei jätäde` ('you [pl.] do not leave'), `es annava` ('they did not give').
- **Quotative mood (East & South Tartu):** Võro-aligned **`-v`** marker (`ollev` 'is said to be', `kirjutev` 'is said to write', `tehtev` 'is said to be done').
- **Impersonal / Passive:**
  - **East Tartu (Võnnu, Kambja):** archaic **personal passive** in 3rd person (`temä kutsutas` 's/he is called' vs. `nemä kutsutaze` 'they are called').
  - **South Tartu:** **`-idi` impersonal past** (`palutidi` 'was burned', `rehitsidi` 'was raked').

## Diagnostic Phonology Affecting Inflection

- **Initial `h-` loss** and non-initial `h` loss (like Mulgi: `ää pere` 'good family').
- **No general non-initial `a`/`ä` → `e` reduction** (unlike Mulgi), except in southernmost Sangaste (`olive`, `käsige`).
- **Q3 lengthening in Q2 forms** (especially West Tartu): `o\li` ('was'), `saanu` ('gotten'), `vanu` ('old pl.p').
- **Short stops after nasals/liquids:** `maald` ('from the land'), `tuulde` ('of winds'), `vigurdega` ('with tricks').
- **Unique Q3 diphthongization of long `üü` → `üi`** (shared only with Helme Mulgi): `müimä` ('to sell'), `püidli` ('fine flour').
- **Long high vowel lowering in Q2:** `oole` ('lips'), `möönü` ('sold').
- **`*eü` reflexes:** North Tartu `ei` (`keids` 'rope', `keitmä` 'to tie'); South Tartu `öi` / `öü` (`löige` 'lukewarm', `löidmä` 'to find').
- **South Tartu (Otepää, Rõngu, Sangaste):** preserves consistent vowel harmony, affricates (`tsuits` 'smoke', `tsile` 'smooth'), and long vowels/diphthongs in secondary-stressed syllables (`inemiisile`, `sugulaisil`).
