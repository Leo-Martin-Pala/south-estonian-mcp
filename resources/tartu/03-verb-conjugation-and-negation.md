# Tartu: Verb Conjugation and Negation

## Present & Past Indicative
1. **3sg Present:** Predominantly **`-p`** in general Tartu (`tulep`, `annap`, `juup`, `kannap`), **`-s`** in s-conjugation (`eläs`, `kõneles`), archaic endingless 3sg (`joo`, `kand`, `om`, `tege`) in southern Kambja, and `-ze` (`aaze`, `jooze`) in Võnnu — avoid Standard Estonian `-b`.
2. **3pl Present:** **`-va` / `-vä`** (`teevä` "they do", `andva`, `tuleva`) or **`-se` / `-sõ`** (`eläse`, `kõnelesõ`), **without** `-vad` or `-q`:
   - Attested (`murdekorpus_tartu`): *"mis nuu sääl teevä‿vai"* ("what those folks do there...").
3. **Simple Past:**
   - 1sg `-i` / `-si` (`olli` "I was", `käisi` "I went")
   - 3pl **`-iva`** (`olliva` "they were", `tulliva` "they came")
   - 3sg palatalized / stem-final (`ol'`, `tull'`, `saie` "got").
4. **Active Past Participle (`-nu` / `-nü` / plural `-nüvä`):**
   - Attested (`murdekorpus_tartu`): *"`kiirdu `lännüvä‿ja"* ("had gone crooked/twisted") — never Estonian `-nud`!
5. **Passive / Impersonal & Conditional:**
   - Impersonal present: **`-tas` / `-täs` / `-das` / `-däs`** (never `-takse/-dakse`); passive past `-t`/`-d`/`-tu`/`-tü` (`anti` past, `tett` part.).
   - Conditional: **`-ss` / `-ssi`** (never `-ksin/-ksime/-ksite`).
6. **Supines & Negation:**
   - Supine inessive: `-man` / `-män` (never `-mas`); abessive `-malda` / `-mäldä` (never `-mata`).
   - Past negation: **`es` + verb** (`es ole`, `es tii`); reject contracted `pole`/`polnud`.
   - *da*-infinitive lacks written `q` (`lüvvä`, `süvvä` in *eki_kiiker_tartu* / *murdekorpus_tartu*).
