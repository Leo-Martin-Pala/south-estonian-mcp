# Tartu Pronouns

## Demonstrative Pronouns

Tartu characteristically uses a **two-way demonstrative system** (with a third medial pronoun in the South group — Otepää, Rõngu, Sangaste). Frequent use of `too` / `tuu` and `nuu` is a hallmark of Tartu speech.

| Distance | Singular | Plural |
|---|---|---|
| **Proximal ('this')** | `sii` ~ `si`, `seo`, `seesinane` | `nii` ~ `ni` ~ `ne` |
| **Distal / abstract ('that')** | `tuu` ~ `tu` / `too` | `nuu` ~ `nu` |
| **Medial ('that there', South Tartu)** | `taa` | — |

## Interrogative & Relative Pronouns

- **Animate ('who'):** `kiä` ~ `kea`; gen. `kenka` / `kel`; part. `keda` ~ `kida` ~ `kidäd` ~ `kidä` ~ `kest`; ill. `kelles(s)`; abl. `kelt`
- **Inanimate ('what'):** `miä` ~ `meh` ~ `midäss` ~ `miga` ~ `migä`; gen. `minga` ~ `mink` ~ `minka`; adessive `mil` ~ `mel`
- **Which of two:** `kumbass` ~ `kumbess` ~ `kumbõss` ~ `kummass`
- **What kind / some:** `määne` ~ `määness` (gen. `määntse`)
- **Compound interrogative + demonstrative:** `kestu` ~ `kõstu` ('who is that?'), `kesnu` ('who are those?')

## Reflexive Pronouns

- `henes` / `enesä` / `esienese` ('self / own'): gen. `enda` ~ `endä` ~ `indä` ~ `hindä`; part. `ennast` ~ `ennest` ~ `endä` ~ `endat`; pl. gen. `endide`; pl. part. `endid` ~ `endit`; com. `ennästega`
