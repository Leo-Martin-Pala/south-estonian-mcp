# Kraasna: Noun Morphology and Cases

## Characteristic Case Endings (Attested Examples)
| Grammatical Feature | Kraasna Marking | Attested Example & Meaning | Contrast with Estonian |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Inessive (`in`)** | **`-h`** (consistently) | `üźäh` in `"Mis sääl naźõ üźäh ol´? Lat´s naźõ üźäh ol´"`, `ḱäeh`, `e̮daguh` | Est. `-s` (shared `-h` with Seto!) |
| **Translative (`into/as`)** | **`-st`** (spoken/EMK; `-s` in folkloric/poetic forms; also unmarked in predictable transitions) | `haìge͔st` ("sick"), `puhti͔st` ("for the funeral"), `vitsavõttijas` | Est. `-ks` (`vitsavõtjaks`) |
| **Illative (`into`)** | `-he`, `-de/-te`, short illative | `Riiiga` in `"Riiiga viiia"` ("Riiga viia") | Est. `-sse` (`-desse/-tesse` flagged) |
| **Elative (`out of`)** | `-st` | `Riiast` in `"Mis Riiast tuuua?"` ("Mida Riiast tuua?") | Shared `-st` |
| **Partitive sg.** | vowel / `-t` | `tˊśalgovikka` in `"kolkümend tˊśalgovikka ańdˊ"` ("kolmkümmend rubla andis") | Governed by numerals `kolkümend` |
| **Diminutive / Collective** | `-kene / -keze` | `velekeze`, `käledzekeź` in `"Ärä vihastelegi i, velekeze / Käritele i käledzekeź"` | Folklore / kinship diminutive |

## Noun & Pronoun Guardrails
- Preserve eastern South Estonian **`-h`** inessive (`üźäh`, `ḱäeh`) and **`-st`** (or folkloric `-s`) translative (`haìge͔st`, `vitsavõttijas`), which closely parallel Seto and Lutsi morphology.
- Do not substitute Standard Estonian `-sse` / `-desse/-tesse` illatives or `-ks` translative (`*-ks`) into Kraasna examples.
- Plural partitive `-sid` and Standard Estonian declensional imports are flagged by the Kraasna leakage linter (`kraasna-leakage-rules.json`).
