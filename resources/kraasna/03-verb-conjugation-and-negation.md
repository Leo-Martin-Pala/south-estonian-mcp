# Kraasna: Verb Conjugation and Negation

## Attested Verbal Features
- **Copula `om` & Past `ol´`:**
  - Attested in `"Mis sääl naźõ üźäh ol´? Lat´s naźõ üźäh ol´"` (`ol´` = 3sg past "was", matching Seto/Võro *oll'*).
- **3sg Past & Stem Alternation:**
  - `ańdˊ` ("andis / gave") in `"kolkümend tˊśalgovikka ańdˊ"` ("kolmkümmend rubla andis").
  - `mõzi` / `mõze` ("pesi / washed") & `soie` / `sui` ("kammis / combed") in `"Mõzi suu, soie pää / Mõze suu, sui är pää"` -> `"Pese suu, kammi pea"`.
- **Imperative & Prohibitive `ärä` + connegative:**
  - `"Ärä vihastelegi i, velekeze"` (`ärä` prohibitive + iterative/reflexive stem `-le-`).
  - Imperative `*Vinnüq vitsavõttijas` ("*Veni vitsavõtjaks"), retaining South Estonian glottal `-q`.
- **Infinitives:**
  - `viiia` ("viia"), `tuuua` ("tuua") in `"Riiiga viiia / Mis Riiast tuuua?"`.
- **3sg Present & 3pl Present:**
  - Endingless 3sg (`om`, `and`) or `-s` (`eläs`); 3pl in `-va(q)/-sõ(q)/omma` (Estonian `-b` and `-vad` are flagged).
- **Negation:**
  - Present: `ei` + connegative (`ei olõq`); Past: **`es`** + verb (`es olõq`), NEVER Standard Estonian `ei + -nud` or `pole/polnud`.
- **Participles, Impersonal & Conditional:**
  - Active past participle `-nu/-nü/-nU(q)` (not Est. `-nud`); passive `-t/-d/-tu/-du` (not Est. `-tud/-dud`); impersonal `-tAs/-dAs` (not Est. `-takse/-dakse`); conditional `-si/-ssi` (not Est. `-ksin`).
