| Case | 1SG | 2SG | 3SG | 1PL | 2PL | 3PL |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Nominative** | `maˀ` | `sa`, `saˀ` | `timä`, `tä` | `mī` | `tī` | `nä` |
| **Genitive** | `mu`, `minu` | `sinu` | `timä` | `mī` | — | — |
| **Partitive** | — | — | `tim̆mä`, `tim̆mǟ` | `meì[d]gi` | — | `näid` |
| **Allative** | `muɫ̆ɫè͔`, `muɫɫè͔` | `suɫ̆ɫè͔` | `timäle`, `tälle`, `täl̆lè` | — | `t́ḕĺe` | `näĭĺe` |

*Note: Allative `-lE` is geminated only in pronouns (`muɫ̆ɫè͔`, `suɫ̆ɫè͔`, `tälle`), not in nouns.*

## Demonstrative Pronouns

- **Distal:** `tū` ('that'), pl. `nu` ('those')
- **Medial:** `tā` ('that (intermediate distance)')
- **Proximal partitive:** `setä` ('this (part.)')

## Interrogative, Indefinite, Reflexive & Reciprocal Pronouns

- **Animate interrogative/relative ('who'):** nom. `kiä` ~ `kià`, part. `kedä`, contracted comitative `kinkka` ('with whom')
- **Inanimate interrogative/relative ('what'):** nom. `mis`, gen. `miä` (?), contracted comitative `minkka` ('with what')
- **Other interrogatives:** `kuis` ('how'), `kunas` ('when'), `paĺĺos` ('how many'), `kos` ('whither', illative), `koh` ('where', inessive)
- **Indefinite:** `kiäki` / `kiäkki` ('someone / anyone'), distributive `egàɫe͔ üt̆tèle` ('to each and every one', all.)
- **Reflexive & possessive:** comitative `hin̆nèga` ('with oneself'), possessive `uma` ('own')
- **Reciprocal:** `ju̇t́ś tē͔źè͔gaˀ` ('with one another', com.)
