# Kraasna: Overview and Orthography

## Identity & Heritage Enclave
- **Language code:** `kraasna` (Kraasna; historically spoken in Kraasna / Krasnogorodskoye language island south of Pskov in Russia, closely related to Seto/Võro).
- **Subgrouping:** Eastern South Estonian enclave preserved in Oskar Kallas's 1903 documentation (*Kraasna maarahvas*), academic phonological studies (*JEFUL 2021*, *Viitso*, *Kask*), EMS/VMS dialect archives, and our corpus collections.
- **MCP Support:** Kraasna lexical records (`eki_vms_dictionary`, `eki_ems_dictionary`), curated academic corpus examples (`kraasna_academic_examples`, `jeful_2021_kraasna_examples`), offline classifier (`kraasna`), cross-dialect cognates, and Kraasna leakage linter (`kraasna-leakage-rules.json`).

## Orthography & Transcription Features in Texts
1. **Historical Fieldwork & Academic Transcriptions:** Texts preserve 19th/20th-century fieldwork and Uralic/academic spellings rather than modern normalized Võro orthography. Do not normalize away historical markers when quoting or citing primary material.
2. **Palatalization Marks:** Acute accents or apostrophes mark palatal consonants very frequently (`ol´`, `Lat´s`, `naźõ`, `üźäh`, `velekeź`, `käledzekeź`, `tˊśalgovikka`, `ańdˊ`, `ḱüĺä`).
3. **Glottal Stop / Laryngeal Notation:** Written inconsistently depending on source (`-q` in `*Vinnüq vitsavõttijas` or `’` / omitted in older archival transcriptions).
4. **Triple/Long Vowels & Archaic Diphthongs:** Historical transcriptions retain long/overlong vowel notations such as `Riiiga viiia`, `tuuua`, `soie pää`.
5. **Vowel Harmony:** Preserves South Estonian back/front vowel harmony (`üźäh`, `velekeze`, `vitsavõttijas`, `laguvama`, `kõder`).
