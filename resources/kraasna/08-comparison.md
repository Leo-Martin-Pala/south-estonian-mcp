# Kraasna Adjective Comparison

## Comparative Suffix

Kraasna attests both `-mb` and `-b` comparative suffixes (as well as `-m` in field notebooks):
- `-mb`: `madalamb` ('lower'), `pikemb` ('taller')
- `-b`: `ińäbät` ('anymore', partitive), `vahneb` ('older'; notebook variant `vahnem`)

## Comparative Constructions

Unlike Latvian-contact Lutsi and Leivu (which developed negative-marker particle comparatives), Kraasna predominantly uses **locational comparatives**, marking the standard of comparison with either the **elative** or the **partitive** (often even within the same sentence):
- **Partitive + elative standard:** `mis on madalamb maa haina ja pikemb muist puist` ('what is lower than meadow grass [part.] and taller than other trees [pl.ela.]')
- **Particle `ku` / `ko` ('than'):** also attested in comparative clauses, without negative-marker calques.

## Inflection
Adjectives inflect for the same case and number categories as nouns (e.g. plural nominative `kuivaˀ` 'dry', translative `haìge͔st` 'sick', comparative partitive `ińäbät`).
