# Kraasna Inflection & Morphology Reference

## Nominal Case System

Kraasna exhibits East Võro / Seto-aligned nominal morphology with grade alternation, vowel-stem plurals, and laryngeal stop (`ˀ` / `q`) marking.

| Case | Marker(s) | Attested Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **Nominative Sg** | Ø | `poig`, `mā`, `käzi` |
| **Nominative Pl** | `-ˀ` (glottal stop, audible even after syncope) | `jut̀ˀ` ('stories'), `tuńńˀ` ('hours'), `käeˀ` ('hands'), `rüäˀ` ('rye') |
| **Genitive Sg** | Ø (grade alternation) | `jumaɫa`, `hobe͔ze͔` |
| **Genitive Pl** | `-i` stem change / `-de` | `rìndu`, `rîh́h́e`, `ɫivvakkaizde͔` |
| **Partitive Sg** | vowel-marked, `-t`/`-d`, or medial gemination | `poìga`, `jumaɫat`, `rahvast`, `v́iert`, `tüt́ä̀rd`, `hame̮ht`, `hobe͔st`; geminated `jim̆mä̀`, `jes̆sä`, `tim̆mä` |
| **Partitive Pl** | strengthened vowel stem; `-it`/`-id` | `rükki` ~ `rüki`, `haìnu`, `kaɫ̆ɫù`, `śäŕḱ̆ḱi`, `uguritsi`; `kapstit`, `sibulit`, `hàm̀bit`, `puid` |
| **Illative** | 1) `-hE` (trisyllabic stems); 2) `-TE` (monosyllabic C-stems); 3) short illative (lengthening) | `koɫkse͔he͔`, `ĺäńkkohe͔`, `keŕkkohe͔`, `huńkkohe`; `riihte͔`, `vīrd́e`; `sûɫa`, `haùda`, `kir̀stu`, `sàǹna`, `me̮t̀sa`, `pat̀ta` |
| **Inessive** | `-h` (consistently) | `ḱäeh` ('in hand'), `e̮daguh` ('in the evening') |
| **Elative** | `-st` | `ah́ost`, `jūst` |
| **Allative** | `-lE` (ungeminated in nouns; geminated in pronouns) | `ɫatse͔le`, `jimäle`, `miheĺe`, `hobe͔ze͔ɫe͔`, `ratt́i͔ɫe͔` |
| **Adessive** | `-l` | `mehèl`, `jiḿs̀eĺ` |
| **Ablative** | `-lt` | `te̮ze͔ɫt` |
| **Translative** | `-st` (also unmarked in predictable transitions) | `haìge͔st` ('sick'), `puhti͔st` ('for the funeral'), `ü̂zest` ('for the night') |
| **Terminative** | `-niˀ` (rare; usually replaced by postpositions `månˀ`, `manuˀ`, `vīrd́e`) | `sēnäniˀ` ('until now') |
| **Abessive** | `-ldAˀ` (in manuscripts) | `-ldAˀ` |
| **Comitative** | `-gaˀ` (no vowel harmony; `-ˀ` often assimilates/elides) | `vīgaˀ`, `kirve͔gaˀ`, `jimä̀ga`, `hobe͔ze͔ga`, `lat̀sigaˀ`, `käśśìga` ~ `käziga` |

### Adjective Comparison
- **Comparative suffix:** `-b` / `-mb` (e.g. `ińäbät` 'anymore (part.)', `vahneb` / `vahnem` 'older', `madalamb` 'lower', `pikemb` 'taller').
- **Standard of comparison:** locational elative or partitive (e.g. `madalamb maa haina ja pikemb muist puist` 'lower than meadow grass and taller than other trees').

## Verbal Morphology

### Non-Finite Forms
- **Infinitive (`*tAk`):** `-dAˀ` (`ki̮ne̮ɫdaˀ`, `maadaˀ`), assimilated/contracted (`nīt̆t́äˀ`, `an̆daˀ`, `min̆näˀ`, `pes̀säˀ`, `kut̀suˀ`, `me̮s̀kaˀ`, `viı̆äˀ`, `tuvvaˀ`, `jel̆lä̆ˀ`, `küt̀täˀ`).
- **Supine (`*mA`):** `-mA` (often geminated after secondary stress: `ji͔stma`, `pidämä`, `magàmmà`, `kuĺat̆tam̆ma`, `ɫāɫat̆tamma`, `ĺeźättäm̆mä`).
- **Active past participle:** `-nU` ~ `-nUˀ` ~ `-nˀ` (`oɫnu` ~ `uoĺnuˀ`, `kuoɫnˀ`, `mânuˀ`, `kündnü`, `väzünüˀ`).
- **Passive past participle:** `-t` / `-TU` in sg.n (`kāb́e͔t`, `pańt`, `ɫāɫattatu`), weakened `-duˀ` in pl.n (`panduˀ`, `jist̀e͔duˀ`).

### Indicative Person & Number
Kraasna preserves both South Estonian conjugations (**Ø-conjugation** and **s-conjugation**):

| Person | Present | Past |
|---|---|---|
| **1SG** | Ø (`ḱȧu`, `mak̆kà`, `ṕeĺgä`) | Ø (`tuĺĺi`, `ji͔s̀te͔`, `ṕeĺksi`, `vei`) |
| **2SG** | `-t` (`nak̆kat`, `leźät̆tät`) | `-ˀ` (`näiˀ`, `käveˀ`, `kūĺˀ`; rare `-t`: `külbset`) |
| **3SG** | Ø (`lät`, `ji͔st`, `ve̮t̀`, `sā`, `tuɫe͔`) / `-s` (`nak̆kas`, `ɫāɫattas`, `jeläs`) | Ø with palatalization (`tuĺ̀`, `kiŕ̀ǵ`, `jüteĺ`, `pańd́`, `vi͔h́t́`, `nak̆kaś`) or `-e` (`mäh̀ke`) |
| **1PL** | Ø (`mī jelä` ~ `jelǟ`) / `-q` | `-q` / `-mi` |
| **2PL** | `-t`, `-dE` (`tī an̆nàde`, `tī istùde`) | `-q` |
| **3PL** | `-vAˀ` (`sāvaˀ`, `lät̀väˀ`) / `-zEˀ` (`kujjoze͔ˀ`, `küzüze`) | `-ˀ` |

### Copula `olema`
- **Present:** 1sg `ole͔`, 3sg `um` ~ `uom` (~ `om`), 3pl `um̆maˀ` ~ `umma` (~ `om̆maˀ`)
- **Past:** 3sg `uoĺ` ~ `oĺ`
- **Connegative:** `ole͔ (e͔iˀ)`; **APP:** `uoĺnuˀ` ~ `oɫnu`

### Mood & Voice
- **Imperative:** 2sg `-ˀ` (`minˀ`, `mińˀ`, `pan`); 2pl `-gEˀ` (`jelägeˀ`, `kuɫɫe͔ɫgeˀ`); negative imperative `är` + connegative (`är võttu=i setä`, `àǹdu ei ärˀ`).
- **Jussive (manuscripts):** `-go` / `-ko`.
- **Passive (impersonal) present:** `-TA` + `-s` (`kǟnt̆täs`, `andas`, `tuvvas`, `müvv́äs`, `vījäs`, `ḱiēdettäs`, `jüldäs`); 3pl passive `pandaze͔ˀ` ('are put').
- **Passive past:** analytic with copula + PPP (`oĺ pańt`, `uom uoĺnuˀ panduˀ`).

### Negation & Clitics
- **Present negation:** primarily postverbal clitic `=eiˀ` with vowel assimilation to the verb stem (`tìĭjä_ei·ˀ`, `oɫe͔ e͔iˀ`, `an̆na_ih`); preverbal `ei` also occurs (`ei saa andaq arq`).
- **Past negation:** postverbal clitic `*es` with vowel assimilation (`oɫnu us`, `jǟ äs`, `lää äs`).
- **Double negation:** attested in both indicative and imperative (`är võttu=i setä`).
- **Emphatic clitic:** `-Ki` (`meì[d]gi`, `jelǟ·gi`, `är̀k̀i`).
