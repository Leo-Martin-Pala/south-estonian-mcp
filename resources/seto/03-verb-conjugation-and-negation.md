# Seto: Verb Conjugation and Negation

## Present Tense & Person/Number
Per Paul Hagu & Karl Pajusalu (*Seto keele teejuht*, 2021, p. 26 & p. 29):
1. **Two Present Conjugations:**
   - **3sg endingless** or **`-(s)s`** depending on conjugation class:
     - `küdsä` ("bakes", transitive) vs `küdsäs` ("bakes/ripens", intransitive)
     - `eläs` ("lives"), `ilotõlõs` ("makes merry"), `sortva'` (3pl "choose/sort")
     - **Trap:** Never read Seto `-s` present forms (`eläs`, `kõnõlõs`) as Standard Estonian past tense!
   - **`om` / `omma'` / `ommava'`:** 3sg `om` ("is"), 3pl `omma'` or `ommava'` ("are").
   - **1sg** is endingless (or stem vowel); **2nd person** varies between `-t` and `-de`/`-dõ`. Infer person/number from the full clause.
2. **Plural Present (`3pl`):** **`-va'` / `-vä'`**, **`-sõ'` / `-se'`** (`sortva'`, `eläse'`, `ommava'`), never Estonian `-vad`.

## Past Tense, Evidentiality, Non-Finite Forms
- **Simple Past Variety:** A single suffix rule CANNOT cover all Seto past verbs! Patterns include:
  - `-i` (`tõi'`, `anni'`, `seledi'`, `kogosi'`)
  - `-(ij)e / -(ij)õ` (`käve` / `k´äve`)
  - `-si` / `-(d)se / -(d)sõ`
  - stem palatalization (`ol'`, `tull'`)
  - or minimal/zero suffix in certain `-u`/`-ü` verbs.
- **Reportative / Evidential Mood:** `-v` / `-vat` / `-vät` and auxiliary-free past participles in narrative can mark reported information — render with Estonian *olevat / kuuldavasti* rather than as directly witnessed fact!
- **Active / Passive Participles:** Active `-nu'`/`-nü'` (not `-nud`); passive `-t`/`-d`, plural `-tu'`/`-du'` (not `-tud`/`-dud`).
- **Characteristic Supine (`ma`-forms) with `-h` / `-st` / `-lda'`:**
  - `tulõmah` = coming / in the act of coming (supine inessive `-mAh`)
  - `tulõmast` = from coming (supine elative `-mAst`)
  - `tulõmalda'` / `elämäldä'` = without coming/living (supine abessive `-mAldA'`) — always preserve the absent-action meaning!

## Negation: Preverbal & Postposed / Fused (Never Strip!)
- **Present:** `ei` / `õi` OR postposed/fused: **`olõ-õi` / `olõ-i`** ("is not"), `võta-ai'` ("does not take").
- **Past:** **`es` / `õs`** OR postposed/fused: **`olõ-õs` / `olõ-s`** ("was not").
- Always inspect trailing `-õi`, `-i`, `-õs`, `-s` after a hyphen before interpreting verb polarity!
