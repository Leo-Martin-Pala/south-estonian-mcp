# Seto: Noun Morphology and Cases

## Number & Glottal Stop
- **Nominative Singular:** Stem or lexical glottal stop (`mõts`, `pühä`).
- **Nominative Plural:** Marked with glottal stop **`-’` / `-q`** (`pühä’`, `väiko’`, `suurõ’`, `saadigu’`, `as´atundja’`), never Estonian `-d`. (Note: in practical prose, apostrophe `'` / `’` is overwhelmingly preferred over `q` in Seto publishing!)

## Core Case Endings (Eastern / Seto Hallmarks Highlighted)
| Case | Seto Ending(s) | Example & Notes | Contrast with Võro / Estonian |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Inessive (in)** | **`-h`** (characteristic!), also `-hn` / `-n` | `kerkoh` ("in church"), `külläh` ("in the village"), `tulõmah` | Core Seto uses **`-h`**; Võro standard uses `-n`; Est. uses `-s` |
| **Translative** | **`-st`** (characteristic!) or `-s` | `suurõst`, `vanõmbast` ("into an elder") | Shared form with elative `-st`; distinguish by verb government! |
| Elative (out of) | `-st` | `plaanõst`, `külläst` | Both elative and translative can be `-st` |
| Illative (into) | short stem / `-he` / `-de` | `moropääle`, `kerkohe`, `tarrõ` | Avoid Estonian `-sse` / `-desse` / `-tesse` |
| Partitive pl | vowel plural, `-sit`, `-it`, `-id`, `-idsi` | `plaanõ`, `mehi`, `näid` | Avoid Estonian `-sid` |
| Allative | `-lõ` / `-le` | `meele`, `talolõ` | Back vowel harmony keeps `-lõ` |
| Adessive / Ablative | `-l` / `-lt` | `moropääle`, `aastagal` | Overlaps with Võro/Estonian |
| Abessive (without) | `-lda'` / `-ldä'` | `rahulda'`, `elämäldä'` | Est. `-ta` |
| Comitative (with) | `-ga` / `-ga'` | `sõbraga'` | No vowel harmony needed on `-ga` |

## Attested Corpus Case Forms (`setomaa_pdf`, `kogukond_setomaa`)
- *"Pühä’ tõi’ meele ilosa ilma ja munaloomka’ moropääle, kohe kogosi’ kokko nii väiko’ ku suurõ’."* ("The holidays brought us good weather and egg-rolling slopes to the yard, where both small and big gathered.")
- *"Pühhi aigu k´auti kerkoh, sääl tä käve."* ("During holidays people went to church (`kerkoh` = inessive `-h`), there he/she went.")
- *"Mõlõmba saadigu’ anni’ ülekaehusõ ummist plaanõst ja seledi’, mille võisi’ õkvalt näid valli’."*
