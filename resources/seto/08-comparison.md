# Seto: Degrees of Comparison (Võrdlusastmed)

## Comparative (Keskvõrre)
- Formed with **`-mb(a) ~ -mp(a)`** (and in Eastern/Southern Seto also **`-b(a) ~ -p(a)`**):
  - `inämb ~ inämp ~ imp ~ inäp ~ inäb` ("more")
  - `noorõmb ~ noorõb` ("younger"), pl `noorõmba’ ~ noorõba’` ("younger ones")
  - `pareb` ("better")
  - `ilosamb` ("more beautiful"), pl `ilosamba’`

## Superlative (Ülivõrre)
- Formed analytically with **`kõgõ ~ kõkõ`** (or `kõˆkõ ~ kõˆgõ ~ kõˆõˆ ~ kõˆigõ`, "most") + comparative:
  - `kõgõ noorõmba’ ~ kõˆkõ noorõba’` ("youngest")
  - `kõgõ ilosamba’` ("most beautiful")
