# Leivu Inflection & Morphology Reference

Leivu is the earliest offshoot of South Estonian (spoken in northeastern Latvia between Alūksne and Gulbene until the 20th century), combining conservative Proto-South Estonian archaisms with extensive Latvian/Latgalian contact features.

## Nominal Morphology

- **Inessive:** `-n` (from South Estonian `*-hnA`, paralleling Old Literary South Estonian, Mulgi, Tartu, West Võro, and Nirza Lutsi).
- **Illative:** analogical `-de` illative (shared areally with Tartu and Hargla Võro) alongside short illatives.
- **Comitative:** `-ga(q)` (shared with the Estonian dialect continuum).
- **Nominative plural:** glottal stop `-q` / `-ʔ` (variable across speakers; clearly preserved in e.g. Jette Uķis's speech: `kan'ebaq`).
- **Word-final consonant clusters preserved:** `kakl` ('neck'), `kakr` ('oat'), `sõpr` ('friend').

### Adjective Comparison
- **Comparative suffix:** `-mb` / `-b` (e.g. `nuorõb` 'younger', `magusamb` 'sweeter').
- **Comparative constructions:**
  1. **Locational elative:** standard in elative.
  2. **Goal-marking `vasta` ('towards') + partitive:** `tuu om kümme aastagut nuorõb vasta minnu` ('s/he is ten years younger than me').
  3. **Neutral particle `ku(i)` ('than').**
  4. **Negative-marker particle `ei` / `is`** (past negation marker) or **negative + 'than'** (`eiq ku`, `eikku`, `e ku`, `isku`), calqued on Latvian `ne` / `nekā`: `küla laib om magusamb eiq ku uma laib` ('guest's bread is sweeter than one's own').

## Verbal Morphology

### Two Conjugations (`Ø`- and `s`-Conjugations)
Leivu preserves the core South Estonian split in active indicative 3rd person:
- **Ø-conjugation:**
  - Prs 3sg: Ø (`tⁱege` 'does', `d́û̬` 'drinks', `š́ü̬̂` 'eats', `tû̬` 'brings', `sâ` 'gets', `lü̬̂` 'hits'; occasional `-p` in monosyllabic vocalic stems `d́ûp`, `š́ü̂p`)
  - Prs 3pl: `-vAʔ` / `-vA` (`tⁱegeväʔ` 'do')
  - Pst 3sg: Ø (`tⁱegi` 'did')
  - Pst 3pl: `-q` or `tⁱekki` (< `*tekihen`)
- **s-conjugation (historical medial/reflexive):**
  - Prs 3sg: `-ss` (`ⁱelass` 'lives')
  - Prs 3pl: `-zEʔ` (`ⁱelazeʔ` 'live')
  - Pst 3sg/3pl: `ⁱelli` ('lived')

### Indicative Person-Indexing

| Person | Present | Past |
|---|---|---|
| **1SG** | Ø (`ma ... anna`) | Ø |
| **2SG** | `-t`, `-dE` (2pl-turned-2sg) | `-t`, Ø |
| **3SG** | Ø (Ø-conj) / `-ss` (s-conj); copula `ᵘom` / `om` | Ø |
| **1PL** | `-mE` / `-m` / `-me` | `-m`, `-me`, Ø |
| **2PL** | `-t`, `-dE` | `-t`, `-de`, `-q` |
| **3PL** | `-vA(ʔ)` / `-zEʔ` | `-q`, Ø |

### Standard Negation
- **Preverbal only** (unlike postverbal Võro/Seto/Lutsi/Kraasna; aligns with Mulgi and Salaca Livonian).
- **Present negation:** `ei` (`ei` + connegative).
- **Past negation:** `is` (`poig is tejja` '[my] son didn't know') — parallel to Livonian `is` / `iz` and Hargla Võro `is`.

### Participles
- **Active past participle:** `-nU` with South Estonian `*kn > *nn` assimilation (`nännü` 'seen' < `*näknüt`).

## Diagnostic Phonology Affecting Inflection

- **No Võro-Seto pre-nasal raising:** retains mid vowels before nasals (`ⁱema` 'mother', `ᵘom` 'is', `sõna` 'word', `uomen` 'tomorrow' — vs. Võro/Seto/Lutsi/Kraasna `imä`, `um`, `syna`, `hummõn`).
- **Short mid-vowel breaking:** `e > ⁱe`, `o > ᵘo` regardless of following consonant (`ⁱeza` 'father', `kᵘoda` 'house', `ᵘoss` 'branch').
- **Overlong mid-vowel raising vs. diphthongization (grade alternation pairs Q3 : Q2):**
  - `i̬i̬ : ie`, `ü̬ü̬ : üö`, `u̬u̬ : uo`, `õ̭õ̭ : õõ` (e.g. `mê̮k̀` : `mê̮ga` / `me̮e͔ga` 'sword').
  - Contracted vowels did **not** raise: `tie` ('do 1sg' < `*tegen`), `vie` ('water gen.' < `*veden`) — vs. Võro `ti̬i̬`, `vi̬i̬`.
- **Diphthong shifts:** regular `eü > äü` (`ḱäüdž` 'rope', `läüdma` 'to find'); Latgalian-influenced `ii > ei` (`lein` 'town'), `uu > ou`, `üü > öü`.
- **Preserved Proto-Finnic archaisms:** `*ai` in `laib` ('bread'), `aim` ('tribe'), `ain` ('hay'), `sain` ('wall'); `*äi` in `säidze` ('seven'), `väitš` ('knife'); affricates `tš`/`dž` (`tšiga` 'pig', `üdži` 'charcoal', `ütš` 'one'); `*nh/*rh > hn/hr` (`vahn` 'old', `tahr` 'enclosure').
- **Prosody:** complex quantity alternations combined with limited stød (broken tone) and voiced plosives (`b, d, g`).
