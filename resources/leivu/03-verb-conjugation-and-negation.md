# Leivu: Verb Conjugation and Negation

## Attested Verbal Features
- **Copula `om` ("is") & Past `olle` ("was"):**
  - `"küla laib om magusa-mb eiq ku uma laib"` ("village bread is sweeter... than own bread")
  - `"vanu-n aju-n olle varbun aid"` ("in old times there was a wicker fence")
  - `"tuu om kümme aastagu-t nuorõ-b vasta minnu"`
- **Necessity / Modal Construction (`ua` + gerund/supine):**
  - Attested parallel (`leivu_academic_examples`): `"nu ua `kiitmise `piehmest"` -> `"Need tuleb pehmeks keeta."`
- **Past / Iterative Forms (`-zi`, `-i`):**
  - `"ku ma zes ikki, zes ma ende ikki un ikki. ku ma zes vauglezi un kuiglezi, zes ma ende vauglezi un kuiglezi"` ("when I cried then..., when I whimpered and howled...")
  - `"peni ka vauglezi un kuiglezi"` ("the dog also whimpered and howled")
- **Conjunction & Adverb Particles in Verbal Clauses:** `un` ("and", Latvian contact), `zes` ("then"), `eiq` (glottal particle/negator), `vasta` ("against / compared to").
- **Standard South Estonian Morphology Boundaries:**
  - Use South Estonian participles (`-nu/-nü/-n` active, `-t/-d/-tu/-du` passive), preverbal **`is`** past negation (`poig is tejja`, paralleling Livonian `is` and Hargla `is`), `-tas/-das` impersonal, and `-ssi/-si` conditional rather than Standard Estonian `-nud`, `-tud/-dud`, `ei + -nud`, `pole/polnud`, `-vad`, `-takse/-dakse`, or `-ksin`.
  - Respect apocopated and palatalized historical transcriptions without normalizing them into modern Võro or Estonian.
