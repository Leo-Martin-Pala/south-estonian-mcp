# Leivu: Noun Morphology and Cases

## Number, Cases & Comparison (Attested Examples)
| Grammatical Feature | Leivu Marking | Attested Example & Meaning | Contrast with Estonian |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Inessive (in)** | **`-n`** | `vanu-n aju-n` ("vanadel aegadel / in old times") | Est. `-s` (`vanadel aegadel` / `-des`) |
| **Translative** | **`-st`** (or `-s`) | `` `piehmest `` in `"nu ua `kiitmise `piehmest"` ("Need tuleb pehmeks keeta") | Est. `-ks` (`pehmeks`) |
| **Partitive sg / pl** | `-t` / `-d` / vowel stems | `aastagu-t` ("years" partitive after numeral `kümme`) | Check numeral government |
| **Comparative Adjective** | **`-mb`** or apocopated **`-b`** | `magusa-mb` ("sweeter"), `nuorõ-b` ("younger") | Est. `-m` (`magusam`, `noorem`) |
| Genitive / Nominative stems | Stem alternation (often shown explicitly in vocab pairs) | `laib` om. `laava` ("bread"), `nińń` om. `ninni` ("flower"), `tätt` om. `tätä` ("father"), `padi` om. `pad´a` ("thick, dense") | Preserve internal stem alternations |

## Pronouns & Demonstratives
- `tuu` ("that, he/she/it"): `"tuu om kümme aastagu-t nuorõ-b vasta minnu"` ("that one is ten years younger than me").
- `minnu` ("me" partitive); `uma` ("own"); `nu` / `nuo` ("need / now", check syntactic slot — `"nu ua `kiitmise `piehmest"` = "need tuleb pehmeks keeta").
- Avoid importing Standard Estonian `-sse`, `-desse/-tesse`, `-sid`, or `-ks` translative into Leivu.
