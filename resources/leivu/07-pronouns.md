# Leivu Pronouns

## Attested Personal & Possessive Pronouns

- **1SG:** nom. `ma`, part. `minnu` (`vasta minnu` 'compared to me'), adessive `mul` (`mul om kan'ebaq` 'I have hemp')
- **2PL:** adessive `täil`, allative `teile` (`ma teile anna` 'I will give you')
- **3SG:** nom. `tä`, `timä`
- **3PL:** nom. `nääq` / `nimäq`
- **Possessive:** `uma` ('own', e.g. `uma laib` 'one's own bread')
- **Reflexive:** `ende` / `enne` (`ennele`, gen. `enda` ~ `endä` ~ `jennõ` ~ `enne`, part. `endat`, `esienese`)

## Demonstratives

- **Distal:** `tuu` ('that', e.g. `tuu om kümme aastagut nuorõb`), pl. `nu` (`nu ua kiitmise piehmest`)
- **Medial / proximal:** `taa` ('this/that')

## Interrogatives & Relatives

- **Animate ('who'):** `keä` ~ `kea` ~ `kie` ~ `k'ee` ~ `kua`; gen. `kinka` / `kelle`; part. `keda`; ade. `kinkal`; com. `kinkaga`
- **Which of two:** `kumbas`
