# Leivu Adjective Comparison

## Comparative Suffix

Leivu forms comparatives with **`-mb`** or apocopated **`-b`**:
- `magusamb` ('sweeter')
- `nuorõb` ('younger')

## Comparative Constructions

Leivu exhibits four attested comparative strategies, combining Finnic locational patterns with Latvian/Latgalian contact constructions:

1. **Locational (elative standard):** standard noun phrase marked in the elative.
2. **Goal-marking `vasta` ('towards') + partitive:**
   - `tuu om kümme aastagut nuorõb vasta minnu` ('s/he is ten years younger than me')
3. **Neutral particle `ku(i)` ('than').**
4. **Negative-marker particle (`ei`, past `is`) and negative + 'than' (`eiq ku`, `eikku`, `e ku`, `isku`)** — calqued on Latvian `ne` / `nekā`:
   - `küla laib om magusamb eiq ku uma laib` ('bread eaten as a guest is sweeter than one's own')
