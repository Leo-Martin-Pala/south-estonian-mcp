## Key Disambiguation Notes
- `padi` in `leivu_eestijuured_vocabulary` means **thick/dense** (`paks, tihe`), not just "pillow".
- `un` and `zes` (`siis`) are normal high-frequency function words in historical Leivu texts, and `d́-` corresponds to Estonian `j-` (`d́alan`, `d́ama`).
- Always verify attested dictionary pairs via `lookup_words(word, lang="leivu")` and `find_cognates(word, lang="leivu")`.
